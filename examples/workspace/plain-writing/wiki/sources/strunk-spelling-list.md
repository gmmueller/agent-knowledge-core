# The Elements of Style: Misspelling

Strunk supplies a compact spelling list and two notes on hyphenation and word division. The list records his chosen forms without supplying a causal theory of why people misspell them. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-misspelling-wikisource-the-free-online]] @ ¶58; [[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-misspelling-wikisource-the-free-online]] @ ¶59)

## Key claims

- Strunk lists accidentally among the spellings to watch. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-misspelling-wikisource-the-free-online]] @ ¶2)
- Strunk lists privilege. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-misspelling-wikisource-the-free-online]] @ ¶4)
- Strunk lists separate. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-misspelling-wikisource-the-free-online]] @ ¶28)
- Strunk lists necessary. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-misspelling-wikisource-the-free-online]] @ ¶36)
- Strunk lists occurred. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-misspelling-wikisource-the-free-online]] @ ¶39)
- Strunk lists parallel. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-misspelling-wikisource-the-free-online]] @ ¶42)
- Strunk prescribes hyphens in to-day, to-night and to-morrow. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-misspelling-wikisource-the-free-online]] @ ¶58)
- Strunk prescribes separate words for any one, every one and some one. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-misspelling-wikisource-the-free-online]] @ ¶59)
