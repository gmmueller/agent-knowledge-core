# The Elements of Style: Matters of Form

Strunk specifies manuscript and scholarly conventions for headings, numbers, parentheses, quotations, references and titles. These are historically situated editorial preferences rather than experimental findings. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-form-wikisource-the-free-online-librar]] @ ¶11; [[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-form-wikisource-the-free-online-librar]] @ ¶32; [[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-form-wikisource-the-free-online-librar]] @ ¶39)

## Key claims

- Strunk leaves space after a manuscript heading. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-form-wikisource-the-free-online-librar]] @ ¶3)
- Strunk writes dates and serial numbers in figures or suitable Roman notation. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-form-wikisource-the-free-online-librar]] @ ¶5)
- Strunk punctuates the sentence outside a parenthesis as if its contents were absent. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-form-wikisource-the-free-online-librar]] @ ¶11)
- Strunk puts a detached parenthetical sentence’s final stop inside the closing mark. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-form-wikisource-the-free-online-librar]] @ ¶14)
- Strunk introduces formal documentary quotations with a colon. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-form-wikisource-the-free-online-librar]] @ ¶16)
- Strunk precedes appositive or direct-object quotations with a comma. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-form-wikisource-the-free-online-librar]] @ ¶18)
- Strunk treats quotations introduced by that as indirect discourse without quotation marks. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-form-wikisource-the-free-online-librar]] @ ¶25)
- Strunk recognizes that periodicals can have different title conventions from scholarly usage. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-form-wikisource-the-free-online-librar]] @ ¶39)

## Quotes

> "Leave a blank line, or its equivalent in space, after the title or heading of a manuscript." — William Strunk Jr. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-form-wikisource-the-free-online-librar]] @ ¶3)

> "Do not spell out dates or other serial numbers." — William Strunk Jr. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-form-wikisource-the-free-online-librar]] @ ¶5)

> "A sentence containing an expression in parenthesis is punctuated, outside of the marks of parenthesis, exactly as if the expression in parenthesis were absent." — William Strunk Jr. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-form-wikisource-the-free-online-librar]] @ ¶11)

> "(When a wholly detached expression or sentence is parenthesized, the final stop comes before the last mark of parenthesis.)" — William Strunk Jr. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-form-wikisource-the-free-online-librar]] @ ¶14)

> "Formal quotations, cited as documentary evidence, are introduced by a colon and enclosed in quotation marks." — William Strunk Jr. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-form-wikisource-the-free-online-librar]] @ ¶16)

> "Quotations grammatically in apposition or the direct objects of verbs are preceded by a comma and enclosed in quotation marks." — William Strunk Jr. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-form-wikisource-the-free-online-librar]] @ ¶18)

> "Quotations introduced by that are regarded as in indirect discourse and not enclosed in quotation marks." — William Strunk Jr. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-form-wikisource-the-free-online-librar]] @ ¶25)

> "For the titles of literary works, scholarly usage prefers italics with capitalized initials." — William Strunk Jr. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-form-wikisource-the-free-online-librar]] @ ¶39)

## Examples

- The third-attempt parenthesis leaves the surrounding sentence’s punctuation intact. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-form-wikisource-the-free-online-librar]] @ ¶12)
- The good-faith parenthesis keeps its own question mark while participating in the outer sentence. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-form-wikisource-the-free-online-librar]] @ ¶13)
