# The Elements of Style: Introduction

William Strunk Jr. presents a short course aid for plain English, rather than an exhaustive theory of literary excellence. His recommendations address recurrent difficulties and leave room for individual instruction and accomplished exceptions. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-introduction-wikisource-the-free-onlin]] @ ¶2; [[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-introduction-wikisource-the-free-onlin]] @ ¶3; [[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-introduction-wikisource-the-free-onlin]] @ ¶16)

## Key claims

- Strunk intends the book for courses combining composition practice with literary study. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-introduction-wikisource-the-free-onlin]] @ ¶2)
- Strunk concentrates on the principal requirements of plain English style. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-introduction-wikisource-the-free-onlin]] @ ¶2)
- Strunk selects frequently violated essentials to lighten instruction and correction. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-introduction-wikisource-the-free-onlin]] @ ¶2)
- Strunk explicitly limits the book to a small portion of English style. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-introduction-wikisource-the-free-onlin]] @ ¶3)
- Strunk recommends instruction based on the problems in the student’s own work after the essentials. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-introduction-wikisource-the-free-onlin]] @ ¶3)
- Strunk recommends Quiller-Couch’s discussion of jargon for further study; their overlap is acknowledged literary influence. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-introduction-wikisource-the-free-onlin]] @ ¶12)
- Strunk allows that strong writers break rhetorical rules to gain a compensating merit. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-introduction-wikisource-the-free-onlin]] @ ¶16)
- Strunk recommends learning everyday plain English before studying literary masters for the secrets of style. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-introduction-wikisource-the-free-onlin]] @ ¶16)
