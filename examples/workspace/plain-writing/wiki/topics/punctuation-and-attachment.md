# Punctuation and attachment

Punctuation and word order express grammatical relationships. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-rules-wikisource-the-free-online-libra]] @ ¶24; [[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-rules-wikisource-the-free-online-libra]] @ ¶29; [[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-rules-wikisource-the-free-online-libra]] @ ¶38)

## Key points

- Strunk distinguishes defining relative clauses from additional parenthetic information; a small parenthetic interruption may omit both commas, but not just one. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-rules-wikisource-the-free-online-libra]] @ ¶24; [[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-rules-wikisource-the-free-online-libra]] @ ¶29; [[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-rules-wikisource-the-free-online-libra]] @ ¶38)
- Strunk permits semicolons or separate sentences between independent clauses without a conjunction, and recognizes short parallel clauses as a comma exception. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-rules-wikisource-the-free-online-libra]] @ ¶60; [[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-rules-wikisource-the-free-online-libra]] @ ¶63; [[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-rules-wikisource-the-free-online-libra]] @ ¶73)
- Strunk attaches an introductory participle to its intended subject and places modifiers to avoid false relationships. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-rules-wikisource-the-free-online-libra]] @ ¶87; [[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-principles-wikisource-the-free-online]] @ ¶227)
