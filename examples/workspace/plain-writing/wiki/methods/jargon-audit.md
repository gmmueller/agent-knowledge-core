# Audit jargon

Look for circumlocution and empty abstraction, then express the actual thought. ([[library/quiller-couch/raw/book/on-the-art-of-writing-selected]] @ p. 121; [[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-misuse-wikisource-the-free-online-libr]] @ ¶2)

## Key points

- Quiller-Couch identifies circumlocution and abstract nouns as two central evasions. Strunk similarly calls for definite statement rather than exchanging vague labels. ([[library/quiller-couch/raw/book/on-the-art-of-writing-selected]] @ p. 121; [[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-misuse-wikisource-the-free-online-libr]] @ ¶2)
- Quiller-Couch’s repetition test and the Fowlers’ structural warning both demand revising the thought’s presentation, beyond simple synonym replacement. ([[library/quiller-couch/raw/book/on-the-art-of-writing-selected]] @ p. 131; [[library/fowler/raw/web/00000000-the-king-s-english-part-1-chapter-1-wikisource-the-free-onli-recapture-20261120]] @ ¶10)
- This tradition has a documented chain of influence: Strunk recommends Quiller-Couch, who in turn borrows a Fowler example. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-introduction-wikisource-the-free-onlin]] @ ¶12; [[library/quiller-couch/raw/book/on-the-art-of-writing-selected]] @ p. 125)

## Procedure

**Inference:** This sequence combines the cited teaching points; the authors do not present this exact checklist.

1. Flag case, nature, character and similar abstract shells, plus roundabout connective phrases.
2. Ask which actor, act or thing the words are meant to name.
3. Rewrite the actual statement and remove any clause that merely repeats it.
4. Recheck technical accuracy and meaning; a necessary technical term is not automatically jargon.
