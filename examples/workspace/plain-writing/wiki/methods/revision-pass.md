# A revision pass

Review meaning, structure and expression in a deliberate sequence. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-introduction-wikisource-the-free-onlin]] @ ¶3; [[library/quiller-couch/raw/book/on-the-art-of-writing-selected]] @ p. 42; [[library/quiller-couch/raw/book/on-the-art-of-writing-selected]] @ p. 56)

## Key points

- Strunk recommends individual work on a student’s actual difficulties. Quiller-Couch regards writing practice as integral to literary study and precise language as clarifying thought. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-introduction-wikisource-the-free-onlin]] @ ¶3; [[library/quiller-couch/raw/book/on-the-art-of-writing-selected]] @ p. 42; [[library/quiller-couch/raw/book/on-the-art-of-writing-selected]] @ p. 56)
- The Fowlers’ abstract sentences may need structural reconstruction; Quiller-Couch’s game-result example needs deletion of a whole repeated thought, not just word swapping. ([[library/fowler/raw/web/00000000-the-king-s-english-part-1-chapter-1-wikisource-the-free-onli-recapture-20261120]] @ ¶10; [[library/quiller-couch/raw/book/on-the-art-of-writing-selected]] @ p. 131)
- Strunk warns that an indefinite original can make the reviser guess at the writer’s intention. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-principles-wikisource-the-free-online]] @ ¶118)

## Procedure

**Inference:** This sequence combines the cited teaching points; the authors do not present this exact checklist.

1. State the intended meaning and flag anything the draft leaves ambiguous.
2. Group the thought into paragraph steps before changing individual words.
3. Find vague labels, repeated assertions and padded constructions; reconstruct the intended statement.
4. Check the revision for lost detail, unwanted emphasis and inappropriate register.
