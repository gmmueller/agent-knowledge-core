# Omit needless words

Keep the detail that earns its place and remove empty verbal padding. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-principles-wikisource-the-free-online]] @ ¶136; [[library/quiller-couch/raw/book/on-the-art-of-writing-selected]] @ p. 121; [[library/quiller-couch/raw/book/on-the-art-of-writing-selected]] @ p. 131; [[library/fowler/raw/web/00000000-the-king-s-english-part-1-chapter-1-wikisource-the-free-onli-recapture-20261120]] @ ¶6)

## Key points

- All three voices target padding: Strunk’s unnecessary parts, Quiller-Couch’s circumlocution and repeated thought, and the Fowlers’ preference for a single word. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-principles-wikisource-the-free-online]] @ ¶136; [[library/quiller-couch/raw/book/on-the-art-of-writing-selected]] @ p. 121; [[library/quiller-couch/raw/book/on-the-art-of-writing-selected]] @ p. 131; [[library/fowler/raw/web/00000000-the-king-s-english-part-1-chapter-1-wikisource-the-free-onli-recapture-20261120]] @ ¶6)
- Strunk explicitly preserves necessary detail; the Fowlers preserve meaning and appropriate register. ([[library/strunk/raw/web/00000000-the-elements-of-style-principles-wikisource-the-free-online]] @ ¶136; [[library/fowler/raw/web/00000000-the-king-s-english-part-1-chapter-1-wikisource-the-free-onli-recapture-20261120]] @ ¶47)
- Quiller-Couch’s instruction to delete a darling targets extraneous fine writing in its immediate context. ([[library/quiller-couch/raw/book/on-the-art-of-writing-selected]] @ p. 297)
